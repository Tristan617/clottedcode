* Reed-Solomon overhead is data-independent — the multiplier is always n⁄k regardless of content.
* RS(n,k): k data shards + (n−k) parity shards. Can recover from any (n−k) lost shards.
* Replication stores full copies; simpler but less space-efficient at equivalent fault tolerance.
